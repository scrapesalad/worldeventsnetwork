World Events Network · Founder & Principal

The person behind the production.

Scott Kerr is an event producer, race director and strategist who turns ambitious ideas into experiences people remember.

Two decades in the field.

With more than two decades as an event producer, Scott has orchestrated a diverse range of large-scale festivals, high-profile running races, concerts, consumer shows, product launches, corporate events, fundraisers, halftime shows and more.

Recent highlights.

His recent work includes directing the 2021 DC Wonder Woman Collegiate Run Series across 17 college campuses, leading the 2023 Rock 'n' Roll Running Series Salt Lake City, and co-producing the 2025 Rockstock Music Festival across six Pacific Northwest minor-league baseball stadiums.

End-to-end leadership.

Scott delivers comprehensive event management for projects of any scale, covering strategy, creative development, budgeting, timeline planning, site selection, vendor coordination, staffing, on-site execution, logistics and post-event wrap-up.

Running & cycling.

Scott directed Utah's premier Salt Lake City Marathon for eight years, drawing more than 10,000 participants annually across a full marathon, half marathon, 5K, bike tour, Kids K, fitness expo and finish line festival.

His race portfolio also includes the Bangkok Midnight Marathon, Phuket Marathon, Las Vegas Marathon, Utah Marathon, Six Flags 5K Race Series, Chicago Half Marathon, The ZERO Run, International Peace Race and Deseret News Marathon.

Beyond the finish line.

His broader production portfolio includes Adventure & Gear Fest, NHL/Celebrity All Star Hockey Game, Miller Daytona Beach Parties, Corporate Games, Oldies Fest Music Festival, Extreme Sports Show, and pre-game ceremonies and halftime shows for College Bowl Games, MLB, NFL, NASCAR and the Indy 500.

The WEN model.

World Events Network pairs senior event leadership with specialized professionals, partners and vendors assembled around the needs of each assignment. The result is a focused team built to make complex events feel clear, connected and ready for the moment that matters.
